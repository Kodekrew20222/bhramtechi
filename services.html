Capabilities

2D & 3D Animation

From concept sketches to fully rigged 3D characters, our animation team builds movement that feels alive — whether it's a stylized 2D explainer or a full CG hero character.

  • Character animation & rigging
  • Explainer and motion-led content
  • Stylized 2D and 3D hybrid workflows
  • Storyboarding and animatics

Visual Effects

Full-CG environments, particle simulations, and invisible effects work that holds up on the big screen and in 4K broadcast delivery.

  • Digital environment builds
  • Fluid, fire & particle simulation
  • Set extensions & digital doubles
  • Invisible / seamless VFX integration

Character Design

Concept art and design systems that give every character — human, creature, or stylized — a consistent visual language across a production.

  • Concept art & character sheets
  • Rigging & deformation setup
  • Style guides for multi-episode consistency
  • Creature and prop design

Pre-Production

Title sequences, brand films, and broadcast graphics built to match a show's tone and hold attention from the first frame.

  • Main & end title sequences
  • Broadcast-ready lower thirds & graphics packages
  • Brand films and explainer motion design
  • Kinetic typography

Production

Where live-action plates and CG elements become one shot — color-matched, grain-matched, and seamless.

  • Multi-pass CG compositing
  • Rotoscoping & keying
  • Color and grain matching
  • Wire and rig removal

Post-Production

Color, finishing, and delivery pipelines tuned for cinema and streaming specs, so a project is festival and broadcast-ready on delivery.

  • Color grading & finishing
  • DCP and streaming-spec delivery
  • QC and technical delivery checks
  • Sound-synced final assembly

How We Work

Four stages, one accountable pipeline.

01

Concept & Previs

Scripts, storyboards, and previs get the shot planned before a single frame is rendered.

02

Production

Animation, VFX, and compositing teams build the shot in parallel, checked against the previs.

03

Review & Iterate

Client review rounds refine every shot until it's frame-accurate to the brief.

04

Delivery

Final color, QC, and format-specific delivery cinema, broadcast, or streaming.
